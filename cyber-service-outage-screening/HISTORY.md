# Scientific revision history

An earlier manuscript asserted empirical logistics-network validation and AUC values of 0.82 and 0.79 for DLRC and k-RCC, against 0.55 to 0.61 for conventional centralities. It also asserted broad prediction of cascade epicenters, near-linear complete-pipeline algorithms, cascade-radius and unmet-demand guarantees, and submodular expected-throughput protection. The available material did not substantiate those experimental claims, and the general protection assertion failed on a series-system example. These claims were withdrawn; they are not results of the present study.

The first computational reconstruction generated 48 independent synthetic physical networks and evaluated 384 single-controller denials. It supplied the inventory LP, selected nominal plans, heuristic and control scores, and a reproducible synthetic record. That experiment did not recover or validate the earlier AUC or empirical-network results. We retain its frozen inputs, archived plans and controller scores for comparison.

The current revision rechecks that record, adds static maximum-flow loss and a delivered-path certificate, enumerates every controller subset, and evaluates joint denial and budgeted primary-failure protection. It adds 18 matched operating conditions, dispatch-plan sensitivity, 12 synthetic cyber configurations on a documented transport benchmark, and seven network sizes through 1024 nodes. Free disposal is made explicit for the constructive path certificate and extended recourse. Core nominal service and single-denial AVI remain unchanged relative to the first reconstruction. The earlier control diagnostics are now exploratory supplemental calculations with revised names and full-reference rank normalization.

The manuscript and code were revised with substantive generative-AI assistance, including formulation, drafting and computational analysis. This history is preserved so that new synthetic evidence is not represented as an original observed dataset or a recovery of missing empirical results.
